## How to agree

There is no separate signing step and no bot. You agree by ticking the CLA
checkbox in the pull request template when you open a PR. Your ticked checkbox,
recorded in the pull request, is the record of agreement.

Maintainers will not merge a pull request where that box is unticked.

If You would prefer not to agree to these terms, say so in the pull request.
We will close it without prejudice — that is a reasonable position, and we would
much rather you raise it before doing the work than after.
